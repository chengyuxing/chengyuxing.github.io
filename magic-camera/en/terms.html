Updated September 21, 2026. Developer: 程钰兴 (Cheng Yuxing).

App license

Magic Camera uses Apple's Standard End User License Agreement. These terms describe app use and Pro benefits and do not limit your consumer rights under applicable law.

One-time purchase, lifetime unlock

Magic Camera Pro is a non-consumable in-app purchase, not a subscription. It does not renew automatically. It unlocks all style filters, intensity adjustment, removal of the free 15-second limit per video, and Pro filters and features added in future versions. Future features may require an app update and compatible hardware and system versions.

The price and currency are the local amounts displayed by the App Store in the app and on Apple's payment confirmation screen. Your Apple receipt shows the amount paid. Pending, cancelled, failed or unverified transactions do not count as completed purchases.

“Lifetime unlock” means that a valid non-consumable purchase does not require recurring renewal. It does not mean unlimited cloud storage, unlimited device performance or guaranteed compatibility with every future operating system. Processing happens on-device; the app does not provide cloud backups of your work.

Restoration and refunds

Purchasers can restore access with the original Apple Account. The app does not require an internet connection every time you open the camera. Purchases, restoration and transaction updates may require an App Store connection. Refunds or revocations update the associated access without deleting existing captures in the app.

Payments, refunds and transaction disputes are handled under Apple's purchase rules and applicable law. Visit Apple Purchase Support.

Capture and keeping your work

Results vary with the device, lighting and scene. Painting filters use traditional image processing, not semantic character redrawing. REC and the timecode in Vintage Tape are part of the visual effect and do not indicate active recording; check the shutter and recording indicator for the actual status.

Captures are stored in the app by default. Export important photos and videos promptly. Deleting the app or local captures, or a device failure, may cause unexported work to be lost. Pro has no commercial per-video time limit, but storage, temperature, battery and system interruptions can still stop recording.
